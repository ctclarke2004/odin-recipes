Peanut Butter Cookies

Peanut Butter cookies with a Hershey's Kiss on them like my dad makes on holidays

These cookies only have 4 ingredients in them and they are very yummy and easy to make

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Sugar
  • 1 Cup Creamy Peanut Butter
  • 1 Large Egg
  • 18 Hershey's Kisses

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 C)
  2. Stir sugar, peanut butter, and egg together in a large bowl until well combined.
  3. Shape dough into 1-inch balls. If dough is too sticky, refrigerate until easy to handle, about 30 minutes.
  4. Place dough balls on an ungreased cookie sheet.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are set and tops are slightly cracked, about 10 minutes.
  6. Press a chocolate kiss into center of each warm cookie. Remove to a wire rack to cool.
